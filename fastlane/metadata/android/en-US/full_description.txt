InstaTube Toolkit by Xboy Rex is a native Android tool for working with public Instagram links and previewing public YouTube video details.

Features:
* Preview public Instagram photos, reels and carousels where the source exposes media
* Choose which carousel items to save
* Copy available captions and extract hashtags or clearly labelled keyword suggestions
* Caption Studio with editable text and copy actions
* Preview YouTube's public title, channel/creator name and thumbnail when available
* Open YouTube videos in the YouTube app or browser, copy links, and save links to your local Library
* Local recent-link history with a clear-history action
* System, light and dark themes; quality and download-folder settings
* Quick URL input from Android's share menu
* No app account, ads, analytics or publisher-operated proxy

YouTube limitation:
This version previews public metadata. It does not download protected YouTube video or audio streams. Duration and view counts are not part of the metadata response used by this version.

Instagram limitation:
Instagram can change its responses or rate-limit requests. Only public media and text exposed by Instagram are available. Private/login-gated posts, Stories, and separate audio extraction are not supported in this build.

Privacy:
Preferences and recent Library records are stored locally. When requested, media links are sent directly to Instagram public endpoints; YouTube video URLs and thumbnails are requested from YouTube services. No app-operated proxy is used.
